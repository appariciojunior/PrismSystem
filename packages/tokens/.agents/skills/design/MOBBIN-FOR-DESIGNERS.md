# Mobbin, in plain language

Mobbin is a library of over 600,000 screenshots from real, shipped apps and websites.
The design suite can search it while you work. Think of it as asking "how have other
products solved this?" and getting evidence back instead of opinions.

## When it helps you

* **Designing something new.** A page or flow your brand has never had. Mobbin shows
 how paywalls, onboarding, saved articles or live blogs behave across many products,
 so your first draft starts from what works, not from a blank page.
* **Generating versions.** When you ask for three directions, Mobbin gives each one a
 genuinely different starting pattern, so the versions differ in approach, not just paint.
* **Evolving a design with a new piece.** Adding a subscription prompt to an existing
 article page? Mobbin informs the new piece; your existing frame leads everything else.
* **Settling a debate.** "Do cancellation flows ask why before confirming?" is answerable
 with evidence in a minute.

## When it does not help

* Checking or scoring an existing design. That is the rules, the tokens and our own
 corpus. Other products are not the yardstick for whether a design is correct.
* Choosing colours, type or spacing. Those always come from our tokens and the DNA.
* Handoff. The spec cites our system, not other people's screens.

## Three rules of thumb

1. **Our own corpus comes first.** If product screens already show the answer, Mobbin is
 a supplement. Own corpus beats Mobbin; Mobbin beats guessing.
2. **Three products make a pattern.** One striking screen is an anecdote.
3. **We learn patterns, never looks.** The suite extracts how a flow works, then rebuilds
 it with your brand's tokens and components. It will refuse "make it look like app X", and
 that is by design: if our page could be mistaken for another product, it has failed.

## Six example prompts

```
/design design a new saved-articles experience. Check Mobbin for how news apps do it.
/design three versions of our subscription paywall, each based on a different pattern
 Mobbin has seen work in news and content products.
/design I'm evolving the article page (Figma is my active tab) to add an inline
 subscription prompt. Ground the prompt in Mobbin prior art.
/design how do subscription apps handle cancellation? Show me the pattern with
 Mobbin evidence before we design ours.
/design a first-run onboarding flow for the puzzles section. Learn from Mobbin,
 but keep it unmistakably on-brand.
/design what do live blogs do about new-update notices? Mobbin evidence plus
 anything in our corpus.
```

## What you get back

Findings with citations, like: "paywalls lead with the article, not the offer
(mobbin · The Guardian · retrieved 2026-07-20)". Every claim names its source, the
same way corpus evidence does, so you can check it and so reviews can trust it.

One practical note: Mobbin needs to be connected (a paid Mobbin account, signed in
once via the browser). If it is not, the suite says so and carries on with our own
corpus rather than making things up.
