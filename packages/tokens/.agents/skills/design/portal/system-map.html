Design System · architecture

The system map

Six levels, one direction of flow, and a learning loop that runs back up the side. Every box states its role; nothing here is decoration.

Read it top to bottom: people ask, the router picks one path, orchestration runs the skills, the skills stand on the grounding, deterministic tools do the maths, and everything produced lands in the sandbox. The green dashed rail on the right is the loop that makes the system better every week.

The brand-specific layer. Swap these five and the whole machine speaks a different brand (that is exactly what rebrand-system does). On any conflict, tokens and the styleguide outrank everything, including the DNA.

The learning loop (the green rail)

Every run appends one line to the week's usage.jsonl → usage-report turns a week of lines into evidence and a friction list → learning-agent evaluates finished sandbox runs against three gates (objective met, critique score, token connectedness) and distils passing runs into lessons → recurring lessons become proposed improvements to skills and rule candidates for the corpus → a human promotes the good ones into the grounding. Nothing edits itself; the loop proposes, people decide. Then the next generation starts from a slightly smarter system.

The three laws that hold it together

Every judgement traces to a source. A finding cites a rule ID and a brand source, or it does not ship. No "feels off".

Semantic tokens only, channel at section level. Raw hex is a bug, palette-direct is a smell, legacy names are drift; components never carry channel colour themselves.

Propose, never self-modify. The corpus and the learning loop suggest; humans promote. The DNA, rules and skills change only by human hand.
