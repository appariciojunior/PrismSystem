# The Design System: One Vocabulary, Every Platform

The Design System translates your brand into digital products. It gives every product stakeholder, designer, and developer a shared vocabulary for building on-brand, consistent digital experiences.

---

## The Mission

The Design System is the single source of truth for every visual decision across all of your products and platforms.

Its goal: to translate your brand into digital experiences that are consistent, accessible, and instantly recognisable. One set of tokens powers every product, every platform, and every device. The result: users feel the same quality and intention everywhere they encounter your products, whether on web, mobile app, or tablet.

---

## Why We Use It

### Brand Coherence

Your users encounter your brand across multiple products, platforms, and touchpoints. Every product should feel like it comes from the same family, and the design system enforces that relationship. One colour change, one typography update, one spacing refinement — and the entire offering feels more refined across every theme, platform, and product automatically. You scale brand impact without multiplying effort.

### Efficiency

Stop rebuilding what already exists. The system provides ready-to-use tokens and components that our team can apply directly in Figma or code. You spend less time on decisions that have already been made well, and more time on the work that actually needs your thinking.

### Consistency

Accessibility and performance standards are baked in, not bolted on. Colour contrast ratios meet WCAG 2.1 AA across all themes and modes. Spacing and typography scales are tested and production-ready. You get a high-quality baseline without having to audit each component yourself.

## Our Guiding Principles

**Built code-first by AI, ready for agentic workflows.**
The system was built using agentic AI, not by exporting from design tools. It's designed from the ground up for programmatic access—AI agents and tools can query tokens directly through the Design System MCP. Design decisions become available everywhere: IDEs, Figma, documentation, AI tools. No manual handoff. No gaps.

**Platform-agnostic, one vocabulary everywhere.**
One set of semantic tokens transforms automatically to web, iOS, Figma variables, and CSS. No manual sync. No platform-specific variants. Change a decision once, and it flows to every platform at once.

**Semantic over literal.**
Tokens describe intent, not implementation. `text.primary` tells you what a token is _for_. A raw value tells you what it looks like today. Semantic naming means the system stays accurate when anything changes—colour, typography, spacing, elevation—and brand expression stays coherent. More importantly, semantics are shared language. When product, design, and code all speak the same vocabulary, there is no translation layer. No loss of intent in handoff.

**Experiment freely, decide deliberately.**
The published Palette layer gives designers space to explore and experiment before semantic intentions are locked in. Early-stage design is about discovery, not constraints. Raw materials let you choose direction first, then map to Semantic tokens later once intent is clear.

## Shape the System

The Design System is a living system. It grows through contributions from designers and developers across your organisation.

We work as a federated model. The design system team maintains the core libraries—the foundations and semantic tokens that power everything. Contributors create extension libraries built entirely on top of these tokens, reviewed and approved by the design system team. This ensures consistency, prevents duplication, and lets teams experiment while staying grounded in shared decisions.
