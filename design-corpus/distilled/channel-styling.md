# Content-section styling in practice (distilled from the corpus)

How each content section (a generic "channel" / accent slot) expresses its colour and editorial tone on real surfaces. Populated by `corpus/distill-corpus`. Cited by `ui/channel-context`, `ui/design-critique` (COL) and `foundation/design-dna` maintenance.

Sections are brand-neutral content-category slots (section-a, section-b, section-c, ...) that map onto the semantic accent tiers (primary, secondary, tertiary), feedback and data-vis charts. There is no fixed newspaper taxonomy.

Each observation carries `(corpus vN, <n> screens)` and links to manifest entries. The core theme tiers and the 1:1 theme rule in `design-dna.md` are the higher authority; this file records how they are used, not what they are.
