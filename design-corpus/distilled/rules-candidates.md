# Rule candidates (proposed by the corpus)

Observations that recur often enough across real screens to be worth encoding as a rule in `foundation/design-rules.md`. Written by `corpus/distill-corpus`; **promoted by a human**. Nothing here counts toward a design score until it is promoted into the design-rules tables with a real ID.

Format:

```
- [ ] <CAT> · <one-line rule> · proposed <error|warning|info> · evidence: <distilled doc>#<anchor> (corpus vN) · <n> screens · <date>
```

Where CAT is one of TYP, COL, SPC, CMP, A11Y, UX, MOT, BRD.
