# Brand Guidelines

## Visual rules
- Lead with the primary colour, use the tertiary sparingly for accents.
- Prefer whitespace over decoration. One idea per surface.
- Corners and spacing follow the token scale, never hand-picked values.

## Writing rules
- Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and headings.
- Short sentences. Cut every word that does not earn its place.
- Speak to the reader as "you", refer to the product by name.
